**Status**: 🟢 Available Now  
**Last Updated**: May 21, 2026

Complete guide to creating, editing, and managing business blueprints.

## What is a Blueprint?

A **Blueprint** is your business foundation document. It contains:
- Business idea and description
- Category and industry
- Timeline and estimated investment
- Status (draft/active/completed)
- AI-generated strategy (when you run agents)

Blueprints are **private to you** — only you can see your blueprints.

---

## Create a Blueprint

### Step 1: Go to Blueprints
1. From dashboard, click **"Blueprints"** in navigation
2. Click **"Create Blueprint"** button
3. Or go directly to `/blueprint/create`

### Step 2: Fill Out Basic Information

**Business Idea** (Required)
- Your business concept in 1-2 sentences
- Examples:
  - "AI-powered email marketing platform for SaaS companies"
  - "Personal branding coaching for entrepreneurs"
  - "Pressure washing service in Houston"

**Description** (Optional but recommended)
- More details about your business
- What problem you solve
- Who your customer is
- Why you're doing this

**Category** (Optional)
- Type of business (Service, Product, SaaS, etc.)
- Helps organize your blueprints
- Drop-down with preset options

**Industry** (Optional)
- Your industry vertical
- Examples: SaaS, E-commerce, Real Estate, Healthcare, Education, Creator Economy, Social Media Influencer, etc.
- Helps agents tailor advice to your niche

**Timeline (weeks)** (Optional)
- How long do you expect to build/launch?
- 12 weeks = default (~3 months)
- Affects roadmap and milestone planning

**Estimated Investment** (Optional)
- Budget for launching/scaling
- Currency: USD
- Affects financial projections and resource recommendations

**Status** (Default: Draft)
- **Draft** - Not launched yet
- **Active** - Currently running
- **Completed** - Finished or archival

### Step 3: Save Blueprint
Click **"Create Blueprint"**

**What happens**:
- Blueprint is created and saved
- You're taken to the blueprint detail page
- You can now edit or run AI agents

**Tier limits**:
- Starter: 3 blueprints
- Pro: 20 blueprints
- Elite: Unlimited
- Founder: Unlimited

## Edit a Blueprint

### Edit Basic Information
1. Open a blueprint
2. Click **"Edit"** button (top right)
3. Make changes to any field
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

**What can you edit?**
- Business idea
- Description
- Category
- Industry
- Timeline
- Investment estimate
- Status

**What can't you edit?**
- Created date (locked)
- Agent results (generated by AI)

### Edit Strategy (AI Results)
- You cannot manually edit agent results
- To refresh: delete the agent result and run the agent again
- Agent results are locked to prevent accidental changes

## Manage Blueprints

### Archive a Blueprint
1. Open blueprint
2. Click **"Edit"**
3. Change status to **"Completed"**
4. Click **"Save"**

Archived blueprints:
- Still appear in your list (with "Completed" badge)
- Can be reopened anytime
- Don't count toward your blueprint limit

### Delete a Blueprint
Deleting is coming soon. For now, mark as "Completed" to archive.

### Organize Blueprints
1. Use **"Category"** and **"Industry"** fields to organize
2. Sort by status (Draft/Active/Completed)
3. Coming soon: Folders and tags for organization

## Best Practices

### For Detailed Agent Results
1. **Provide complete information**
   - Fill in all fields (especially description)
   - More detail = better AI results

2. **Be specific about industry**
   - Choose the closest industry
   - Affects how agents tailor advice

3. **Realistic timeline**
   - Set honest launch timeline
   - Affects roadmap milestones

4. **Reasonable budget**
   - Estimate startup costs
   - Affects resource recommendations

### Blueprint Naming
- Use clear, descriptive names
- Examples: "AI Marketing Platform", "Pressure Washing Houston"
- Avoid vague names like "Business 1" or "Startup"

### Multiple Blueprints
- Create separate blueprints for different businesses
- Compare strategies across ideas
- Run all agents on multiple ideas to find best market fit

## Troubleshooting

### Blueprint Won't Save

**"Error saving blueprint"**
1. Check you filled in the Business Idea field (required)
2. Refresh the page
3. Try again

**Changes disappeared after refresh**
1. Click **"Edit"** button
2. Make changes
3. Click **"Save Changes"** (don't just refresh)

### Agent Won't Run

**"Agent available for Pro and Elite tiers only"**
- Upgrade to Pro or Elite tier to run agents
- See [Tier System](./HELP_TIER_SYSTEM.md)

**"Blueprint not found"**
- Your blueprint may have been deleted
- Go back to Blueprints list
- Create a new blueprint

**"Agent is taking too long"**
- Normal: 2-3 minutes
- Give it time to process
- Don't refresh or close the page
- If >5 minutes, refresh and check status

### Can't See Agent Results

**Results display is blank**
1. Refresh the page
2. Wait a few seconds
3. Scroll down to see results

**Results appear formatted strangely**
1. This is normal for large JSON blocks
2. Click section headers to expand/collapse
3. Use your browser's zoom (Ctrl +/-) to adjust size

### Hitting Blueprint Limit

**"You've reached your blueprint limit"**
- Starter: 3 blueprints max
- Archive or complete old blueprints
- Upgrade to Pro (20) or Elite (unlimited)

## FAQ

**Q: Can I share my blueprint with someone?**
A: Coming soon. Right now blueprints are private.

**Q: Can I export my blueprint as PDF?**
A: Coming soon. For now, you can copy/paste or screenshot.

**Q: What if I run an agent twice on the same blueprint?**
A: New results overwrite the old ones. Agent results are not versioned yet.

**Q: Can I see who viewed my blueprint?**
A: Blueprints are private to you only. No one else can see them.

**Q: How long are agent results saved?**
A: Permanently. As long as you keep the blueprint, results are saved.

**Q: Can I change my business idea after creating a blueprint?**
A: Yes. Click Edit and update any field. Old agent results stay the same (you'd need to re-run agents to update).
