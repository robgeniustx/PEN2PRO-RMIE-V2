## Create Your Account (Sign Up)

### Step 1: Navigate to Sign Up
Go to the PEN2PRO website and click **"Sign Up"** or visit `/signup`

### Step 2: Enter Your Details
Fill out the registration form:

**Required Fields**:
- **Email Address** - Your email (used for login)
- **Password** - Minimum 8 characters (use a strong password)
- **Confirm Password** - Repeat your password to confirm
- **Accept Terms** - Check the box to agree to terms of service

**Tips for strong password**:
- Mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
- Include numbers (0-9)
- Include special characters (!@#$%^&*)
- Avoid personal information (birthdate, name, etc.)
- Minimum 8 characters (longer is better)

### Step 3: Complete Registration
Click **"Create Account"**

**What happens**:
- Account is created immediately
- You're automatically logged in
- You're assigned **Starter tier** (free access)
- You can create up to 3 blueprints
- You can view (but not run) AI agents

### Step 4: Verify Email (Recommended)
Check your email for a verification link. Click it to verify your email address.

**Why verify?**
- Recover your account if you forget your password
- Receive important updates
- Unlock additional features (coming soon)

## Manage Your Account

### Access Your Profile
1. Click your profile icon (top right of app)

View and edit:

1. Click profile icon → **"Settings"**
2. Or navigate to `/settings`

**Available in Settings**:
- Profile information
- Password change
- Email preferences
- Account tier details
- Billing information (coming soon)

## Change Your Password

### Method 1: From Settings (Logged In)
1. Go to **Settings** → **Password**
2. Enter your **current password**
3. Enter your **new password** (twice to confirm)
4. Click **"Update Password"**

**Password must**:
- Be at least 8 characters
- Be different from current password
- Use mixed case and numbers/symbols for security

### Method 2: Forgot Password

If you're locked out:

1. Go to **Log In** page
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Enter your **email address**
4. Check your email for a **reset link**
5. Click the link (valid for 24 hours)
6. Enter your **new password** (twice)
7. Click **"Reset Password"**

**If you don't see the email**:
- Check spam/junk folder
- Wait 5 minutes and try again
- Make sure you're using the correct email

## Security & Privacy

### Keep Your Account Safe
1. **Use a strong password** (8+ characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols)
2. **Don't share your password** with anyone
3. **Use unique passwords** (don't reuse across sites)
4. **Enable two-factor authentication** (coming soon)
5. **Log out** after each session (especially on shared devices)

### Data Privacy
- **Your blueprints** are private (only you can view)
- **Your agent results** are not shared
- **Your email** is never sold or shared
- **Encryption** protects your data in transit

See our **Privacy Policy** for complete details.

### Log Out
1. Click your **profile icon** (top right)
2. Select **"Log Out"**
3. You'll be logged out immediately

To log out everywhere:
- Go to **Settings** → **Security**
- Click **"Log Out of All Sessions"**
- You'll need to log in again on each device

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Log In

**"Invalid email or password"**
- Check that you entered the correct email
- Check that you entered your password correctly
- Passwords are case-sensitive
- Make sure CAPS LOCK is off

**"Too many login attempts"**
- You've tried to log in too many times
- Wait 15 minutes and try again
- Use the "Forgot Password" link to reset

**"Account doesn't exist"**
- You haven't signed up yet → [Sign up here](#create-your-account-sign-up)
- You created an account with a different email → try other emails
- Your account may have been deleted → sign up again

### Forgot My Password

1. Go to **Log In**
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Enter your email
4. Check email (including spam folder) for reset link
5. Click link and create new password
6. Log in with new password

**Reset link not working?**
- Link expires after 24 hours (get a new one)
- Links are one-time use only
- If browser shows error, try private/incognito mode

### Profile Won't Update

**Changes didn't save?**
1. Check you clicked **"Save"** button
2. Look for error messages in red text
3. Fix any validation errors (email format, name length, etc.)
4. Try again

**Still not saving?**
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Log out and log back in

### Can't Upgrade Tier

**Coming soon**: Stripe integration

**In the meantime**:
- Contact support to upgrade manually
- We'll activate your tier immediately

### Password Reset Isn't Working

**I didn't receive the reset email**:
1. Check spam/junk folder
2. Check that email is correct
3. Try again (wait 5 minutes first)
4. Contact support if persistent

**Link says "Invalid or expired"**:
1. Get a new reset link
2. Reset links expire after 24 hours
3. Try a different browser or private mode

**Q: Can I delete my account?**

**Q: What happens if I don't log in for a long time?**
A: Nothing. Your account never expires. Log in anytime.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
